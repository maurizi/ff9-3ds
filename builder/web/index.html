FF9 3DS Builder

Turns your own four Final Fantasy IX (NTSC-U) discs into the 3DS game, entirely in this page: your disc images are read on your computer and never uploaded.

1. Your discs

 BIN/CUE, IMG/CCD or raw ISO: select the sheets and their images together.

    2. What to make

    Add your four discs to begin.

    About 1.7 GB is written to the browser's storage while it builds (cleared afterwards), and the build takes several minutes. The file it makes holds your copy of the game: keep it to yourself.
